How it works

1. Rate every team. Start from win percentage, pulled toward .500 (a 7-1 start partly reflects luck). Convert that to a strength score on the log-odds scale.

2. Price each game. Win probability = logistic(home strength − away strength + home-field edge).

3. Simulate the season. Flip a weighted coin for every remaining game. Games not yet on the schedule below are played against a league-average opponent.

4. Add uncertainty. Each simulated season, team strengths get random noise, because we don't know any team's true strength.

5. Seed the playoffs. 4 division winners + 3 wild cards per conference, then simulate the bracket through the Super Bowl.

6. Repeat 10,000 times and count. If a team makes the playoffs in 6,200 seasons, that's 62%.

Simplifications: ties in the standings are broken by coin flip (not official NFL tiebreakers); no injuries or rest; ties in individual games aren't simulated.
